Playback Loop

First-person puzzle game by MapPack. Record your moves and replay them as ghosts.

About the game

Playback Loop is a puzzle platformer built around time. Record what you do, then replay it as a ghost and work alongside your past selves, or against them. Most puzzles have more than one solution, so planning a few moves ahead pays off.

  • Record and replay your own actions to solve puzzles
  • Flexible mechanics that allow several solutions
  • Steam achievements, stats and leaderboards
  • Full audio and subtitles in English
